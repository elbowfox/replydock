# Chrome Web Store listing

Name: ReplyDock
Short description: Insert saved replies and variables into any text field.

Long description:

ReplyDock keeps the messages you send every day and inserts them where you are already typing.

Use it for support replies, sales follow-ups, billing notes, and founder updates. Search your library, fill variables like {{name}} or {{amount}}, and insert into the focused field.

Free includes 10 replies. Pro removes the limit with a license key you paste in Settings. Templates stay on your computer. ReplyDock does not upload page content or replies.

Category: Productivity
Language: English
Permission justification: storage is for your template library and license. activeTab and scripting are used only when you click Insert, to place text in the field you focused.

Single purpose: insert user-saved reply templates into the current page.
