Install

  1. Unzip the extension folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Load unpacked and choose the replydock folder.
  4. For the public listing, zip the folder and upload it to the Chrome Web Store.

Privacy

ReplyDock stores templates, variables, and the license key in Chrome local storage on your machine. It does not send replies, page contents, or keys to a server. Insert works only after you click Insert, on the tab you are using. There is no account and no analytics in this build.

ReplyDock is a local tool. A license key is a purchase receipt, not a subscription unless you choose to sell it that way.
